Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Near Miss: Dodge the Dots (the “App”), developed by Buyoun Lim (“I”, “me”), handles information.

1. Information I collect

I do not collect, store, or receive any personal information myself. The App has no accounts or sign-in. Your best scores and settings (such as the selected difficulty) are stored only on your device and are deleted when you delete the App.

2. Advertising

The App shows ads: rewarded video ads that you choose to watch to continue a run, and occasional full-screen ads between games. Ads are provided through Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service of Unity Technologies, including the ironSource and Unity Ads networks.

To deliver ads, measure their performance, and prevent fraud, these services may automatically collect the following from your device:

  • Device identifiers: the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), only if you allow tracking, the Identifier for Vendors (IDFV), and identifiers assigned by the ad services
  • IP address, and approximate (country-level) location derived from it
  • Device information such as model, operating system version, language, time zone, and network type
  • Ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown, clicked, or watched to the end
  • Performance data, such as ad loading times

This information is collected and processed by Unity Technologies under its own privacy policy:

  • Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy
  • Unity Privacy Hub

3. Tracking (App Tracking Transparency)

When you first open the App, iOS asks whether you allow the App to track your activity. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, your advertising identifier (IDFA) is not shared and the ads you see will be less personalized. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

4. Unity engine

The App is built with the Unity engine, which may send anonymous technical information about your device (such as model, operating system version, and hardware capabilities) to Unity Technologies to help improve the engine.

5. International processing

Information collected by Unity Technologies may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (under 14 in Korea), and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Retention, deletion, and your rights

Because I do not hold any of your personal information, there is nothing for me to delete. Data stored on your device is removed when you delete the App. To access or delete information held by Unity Technologies, follow the instructions in Unity's privacy policy or contact Unity at DPO@unity3d.com.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I will update this page and the effective date above.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, please email devfreedomlabs@gmail.com.
